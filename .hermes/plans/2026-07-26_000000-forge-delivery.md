## Context

Forge is a fork of [Libra AI](https://github.com/nextify-limited/libra) — an open-source V0/Lovable alternative. The tech team uses it to scaffold, prototype, and ship internal tools faster.

**Stack:** Bun + Turborepo monorepo, Next.js (OpenNext on Cloudflare Workers), tRPC, Drizzle ORM, `better-auth`, Cloudflare D1/KV/R2.

**What we're ripping out:** Stripe/billing, PostHog, GitHub OAuth, all plan-gating UI.

**What we're keeping:** Email OTP auth (restricted to `@travelresorts.com`), e2b sandboxes, Anthropic Claude as the AI, full IDE/deploy flow.

**What we're adding:** Domain restriction on OTP, Forge branding, `forge.travelresorts.com` routing, internal "everyone is admin" plan.
